# ARS Green Steel Sitemap

## Purpose

This sitemap defines the core pages for the ARS Green Steel website using the approved Homepage V1 as the reference point.

The site structure should support:

- Trust building through proof, certifications, manufacturing strength, and product clarity.
- Enquiry generation through quote, contact, dealer, calculator, and price flows.
- Clear journeys for home owners, engineers, contractors, dealers, and procurement teams.

## 1. Home

URL:

- Route visitors quickly into product, price, calculator, dealer, and enquiry journeys.
- Introduce the full site experience through proof, products, audience paths, quality, and contact.

Target User:

- Home owners
- Engineers and architects
- Contractors

## Strategic Note

The ARS sitemap should move users through a simple decision path:

1. Understand the brand.
2. See proof.
3. Choose product or application.
4. Use buying tools.
5. Contact, find a dealer, or submit an enquiry.
